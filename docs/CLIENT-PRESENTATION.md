**"Can we do it cheaper?"**  
"The ₹25,000 core website covers design, development, and all features. Below that, we'd have to remove sections like Panchang, Kundli, or blog — which are what bring daily visitors and Google traffic."

**"Why not use Wix/WordPress myself?"**  
"You could, but astrology sites need specific tools — Kundli forms, Panchang, WhatsApp booking, Hindi-English content, mobile optimization. Building this yourself takes months. I deliver in 3 weeks with ongoing support."

**"Do I need monthly maintenance?"**  
"Technically no, but websites need updates for security and Google ranking. One hack or downtime during a festival season could cost more in lost bookings than a year of maintenance."

**"What about AstroTalk — they have an app?"**  
"AstroTalk takes 20–30% commission and lists you among thousands. Your own website is 100% yours — no commission, full brand control, direct client relationship."
